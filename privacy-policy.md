# Privacy Policy for PDF Tool +

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Developer:** Dark MysteriX

PDF Tool + ("the Application") is a standalone offline desktop utility for Windows. Your documents and personal data remain strictly on your device.

## 1. 100% Offline Local Processing
* **Local Execution:** All PDF operations—including viewing, organizing, merging, splitting, cropping, text editing, watermarking, signing, OCR, compression, comparison, and AES encryption—run 100% locally on your PC.
* **No Cloud Uploads:** Your files, images, and extracted text are never uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party networks.
* **Temporary Data:** Undo/redo histories and rendering caches stay in local memory or standard Windows temporary folders and are cleared when your session closes.

## 2. Information Collection & Storage
* The Application requires no account, login, or registration.
* We do not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal information (PII), analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
* App preferences (theme, font size, save-name format, recent file paths, and optional session tabs) are stored locally on your PC in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFToolPlus\settings.ini`.

## 3. In-App Purchases
* Optional Pro upgrades and license verification are processed directly by the Microsoft Store commerce platform under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. Dark MysteriX does not receive or store your payment details.

## 4. Third-Party Open-Source Engines
* PDF Tool + uses local open-source components (PySide6, PyMuPDF, Tesseract OCR, Ghostscript, LibreOffice) strictly for offline processing. None transmit user files externally.
