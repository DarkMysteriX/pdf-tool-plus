Privacy Policy for PDF Tool +

Last Updated: November 2026  ·  Developer: Dark MysteriX

PDF Tool + ("the Application") is a standalone offline desktop utility for Windows developed by Dark MysteriX. Your documents and personal data remain strictly on your device.

1. 100% Offline Local Processing

  • Local Execution: All PDF operations—including viewing, organizing, merging, splitting, cropping, text editing, watermarking, signing, OCR, compression, comparison, and AES encryption—run 100% locally on your PC.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Your PDF files, images, and extracted text are never uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party networks.
  • Temporary Data: Undo/redo histories and rendering caches stay in local memory or standard Windows temporary folders and are cleared when your session closes.

2. Information Collection & Storage

  • The Application requires no account, login, or registration.
  • We do not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal information (PII), analytics, telemetry, ads, or crash reports.
  • App preferences (theme, font size, save-name format, recent file paths, and optional session tabs) are stored locally on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFToolPlus\settings.ini.

3. In-App Purchases

  • Optional Pro upgrades and license verification are handled directly by the Microsoft Store commerce platform under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. Dark MysteriX does not receive or store your payment details.

4. Open-Source Local Engines

  • PDF Tool + uses local open-source components (PySide6, PyMuPDF, Tesseract OCR, Ghostscript, LibreOffice) strictly for offline document processing. None transmit user files externally.

5. Children's Privacy

Because PDF Tool + does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children of any age.
